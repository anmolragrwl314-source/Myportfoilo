About

I'm a computer science student at Lovely Professional University with a strong focus on Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence. What excites me most is turning ideas into working systems — I've built everything from robots that navigate on their own to secure tools for storing sensitive notes and platforms that let people collaborate in real time.

My toolkit spans Python, Java, and C++, along with full-stack development using React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL.
